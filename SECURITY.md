# Security policy

sc4n reads untrusted bytes and reports what they would do. A defect in that
reading is a security defect in the scanner: a false negative reads exactly like
a clean file, and a cap that stops the walk silently is indistinguishable from
an artifact with nothing in it.

## Supported versions

The latest `v1.0.x` release. Fixes land on `main` and ship in the next tag;
pre-releases (`v1.0.1-rc1`) exist for testing and are not supported. Older
releases are not backported.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: **Security → Report a
vulnerability** in this repository
(<https://github.com/fr0g-66723067-io/sc4n/security/advisories/new>). The report
stays private until an advisory is published, and it carries the discussion,
reproducer and fix in one place.

If that channel is unavailable, email <fr0g@66723067.io> with `sc4n security` in
the subject. No project PGP key is published; encrypt only with a key you have
verified out of band.

A useful report states:

- the version the test ran against (`sc4n version`, or the image tag);
- the exact command and flags;
- the input bytes, or the smallest artifact that reproduces the behaviour
  (generated bytes are better than a third-party model you may not share);
- what you expected and what the scanner reported instead;
- whether you intend to publish, and on what timeline.

Do not attach production credentials, private models, or artifacts you are not
allowed to redistribute.

We aim to acknowledge within seven days and to agree a disclosure date with the
reporter. This is a best-effort project: there is no bug bounty and no service
level beyond that acknowledgement. If a report is a documented limitation rather
than a defect, the answer will cite the document that states it.

## In scope

- **A silent miss.** A format the parser claims to cover is parsed partially or
  not at all without an `SC4N-LIMIT-*`, `SC4N-PARSE-FAILED`,
  `SC4N-PARTIAL-SLICE` or `SC4N-ARCHIVE-UNSUPPORTED-COMPRESSION` finding. The
  same applies to a rule that stops firing on a shape it was written for.
- **A false negative with a security consequence.** Bytes that load and execute
  through a documented loader while the scan reports nothing at or above the
  rule's stated severity.
- **Resource exhaustion.** Crafted bytes drive unbounded memory or CPU, or a cap
  in `docs/architecture.md` does not fire and does not report itself.
- **Report injection.** Artifact-controlled text escapes the text table,
  corrupts the JSON, SARIF or CycloneDX structure, or forges a baseline entry.
- **Path escape.** `scan` reads a file outside the scanned tree or the `-root`
  it was given, including through a symlink, an archive member or an ONNX
  external-data reference.
- **Network from the scan path.** Any dial made by `sc4n scan`; only `sc4n hub`
  is allowed to use the network.
- **Sandbox or attestation defects in `tools/sc4nconfirm`.** A payload that
  escapes the container flags, blinds `strace`, or produces an attestation the
  scanner accepts for bytes it did not demonstrate.
- **Release integrity.** A published asset that does not match `SHA256SUMS`, a
  workflow that publishes from a branch, or an artifact built from other bytes
  than the tag.

## Out of scope

- A malicious artifact that sc4n reports correctly. That is the scanner doing
  its job; triage guidance is in [`docs/triage.md`](docs/triage.md).
- Formats the documentation says are recognised but not deep-parsed (xz, lz4,
  zstd, 7z, rar; TensorRT sections; the Core ML ML Program graph), and the
  coverage limits stated in [`docs/threat-model.md`](docs/threat-model.md) §11.
- Weight-level backdoors and the statistical rules behind `-deep-stats`: they
  are informational by design, with the false-positive caveats in
  [`docs/threat-model.md`](docs/threat-model.md) §6.
- Vulnerabilities in the loaders sc4n models (torch, pickle, Keras, TensorFlow,
  Jinja2, …). Report those to the project that ships the loader.
- Scanning an artifact larger than the machine's own resource budget: use the
  caps, the container, or a bigger machine.
- Missing a finding because a rule deliberately trades recall for precision; the
  rule's confidence and `docs/triage.md` say so.

## Safe harbour

Testing is welcome against artifacts you own or are authorised to test, and
against a local copy of this repository. Do not point `sc4n hub` at third-party
repositories in a way that violates their terms, and do not upload other
people's private artifacts into a report.

Reports are credited by name or handle unless you ask otherwise.
